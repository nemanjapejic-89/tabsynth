Tab Synth Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

Summary: Tab Synth processes your tab content only when you click "Synthesize". We don't store, track, or sell your data. Everything happens on-demand with your explicit consent.

What Data We Process

When you use Tab Synth, we temporarily process:

  • Tab titles and URLs - To identify your selected tabs
  • Page content - Text extracted from selected tabs (max 1,500 characters per tab)

This data is sent to our secure API solely to generate your synthesis and is not stored after processing.

What We Don't Collect

  • Personal information (name, email, etc.)
  • Browsing history
  • Cookies or tracking data
  • Analytics or usage patterns
  • Any data from tabs you don't explicitly select

Data Storage

Tab Synth stores minimal data locally in your browser:

  • Consent status - Whether you've accepted this privacy policy
  • Daily quota count - Number of syntheses used today (resets at midnight UTC)

This data never leaves your device and can be cleared by removing the extension.

Third-Party Services

Tab Synth uses:

  • Groq API - To generate AI-powered syntheses. Content is processed but not stored. See Groq's Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudflare Workers - To securely route requests. No logging enabled. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

User Control

You have full control:

  • Opt-in only - Nothing happens until you click "Synthesize"
  • Selective processing - Only tabs you check are processed
  • Decline option - You can decline consent and the extension won't process any data
  • Easy removal - Uninstall anytime to remove all local data

GDPR Compliance

For users in the European Economic Area:

  • We process data based on your explicit consent
  • You can withdraw consent by declining in the extension or uninstalling
  • No personal data is retained after processing
  • No data is transferred for marketing or profiling

Children's Privacy

Tab Synth is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, we'll increment the consent version in the extension, prompting you to review and accept the changes.
